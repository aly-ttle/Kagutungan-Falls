How to get there

From Santa Cruz to the falls

Getting to Kagutungan Falls has two parts: reaching Barangay Daligan by road, then following the trail on foot. Budget half a day for the round trip.

By commute

  1. Ride a bus toward Vigan or Laoag

    From Manila or nearby provinces, take a bus heading north along the Ilocos route and ask the conductor to drop you off at the Santa Cruz Public Market.

  2. Charter a tricycle to Barangay Daligan

    From the public market, hire a whole tricycle going to Barangay Daligan. Drivers around the market are familiar with the trip.

  3. Register at the basketball court

    On arrival in Barangay Daligan, sign in at the basketball court before heading to the trailhead. A local tour guide is optional here.

  4. Trek to the falls

    Follow the trail east, crossing the Kagutungan river and walking along the irrigation canal path. The walk takes roughly 30 minutes at an easy pace.

By private vehicle

  1. Drive to Santa Cruz, Ilocos Sur

    Head to the town proper of Santa Cruz along the national highway.

  2. Turn toward Barangay Daligan

    From the highway, Barangay Daligan is roughly 5.8 kilometers inland to the east. Ask locals for directions if unsure, since the turning point is easy to miss.

  3. Park and register at the basketball court

    Sign in at the barangay basketball court before starting the trek. This is also where a tour guide can be arranged if you'd like one.

  4. Trek to the falls

    Same trail as the commute route: about 20 minutes on foot, crossing the river along the way.

Trail Notes and Safety Reminders

The trail crosses the river and can be uneven or overgrown in places, so wear closed shoes with good grip. Vegetation along the path has been known to cause skin irritation for some visitors — long sleeves and pants are worth considering.

Although Kagutungan Falls is beautiful, visitors should exercise caution:

  • Avoid diving into unfamiliar parts of the pool.
  • Wear footwear with good traction, especially after rain.
  • Supervise children at all times.
  • Follow local guidelines and keep the area clean.
  • Swimming should be avoided during periods of strong current or bad weather.
Contact the barangay tourism desk beforehand if you'd like to arrange a guide.
